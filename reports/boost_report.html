What we found

  1. 1Impressions is the paid metric that matches the platform count.
  2. 2Subtracting paid from the total does not work for one post.
  3. 3The views before the boost, grown at the normal organic rate, do work.
  4. 4The post-ID tag in the paid table is the best proof that a post is paid.
  5. 5The model meets every target on TikTok. On Instagram, a flag is reliable but an organic call is not proof.
CaveatTikTok organic after a boost is an estimate, not verified.

FAQ

How did we test “total − paid = organic”?

We matched 159 paid Instagram posts to their Meta ads and lined up, day by day, public views, opt-in views (organic only), SocAPI plays and every paid metric by placement. 13,859 post-days, 2025-09-18 to 2026-10-07.

Why does subtraction fail?

Organic is the small number left after you remove a large paid number. Paid is a median 94% of public views. On the 10 tagged posts while ads ran, 47% of days give negative organic.

What about TikTok and YouTube?

TikTok public views rise 1.17× the paid plays (512 posts). But TikTok opt-in equals public (ratio 1.000), so there is no organic truth after a boost. Subtraction gives 5.19× the curve estimate, and we cannot check which is right.

YouTube has no ad-to-video link in the warehouse (0 of 34,687 posts), so it cannot be tested.

How do we know a post is paid?

Each post gets its strongest proof. The model only scores posts with none.

Gold check: our Meta ad-name rule finds 21 of 21 tagged posts. The TikTok Spark link finds 0 of 3 tagged campaign posts, so the tag goes first. One TikTok test “false positive” is a tagged paid post: the label was wrong, the model was right.

How was the model kept honest?
  • No paid data in the inputs: only public views, likes, comments, shares and followers up to the model day (14, 30 or 60), and the same creator's earlier posts.
  • Targets written down before any test (docs/IMPROVEMENT_PLAN.md). Every change was chosen on train only and logged there first.
  • Locked test (posts 2026-07-01 to 2026-09-09): v1 was scored on it once, v2 once more (second look). Fresh posts (2026-09-10 to 2026-09-24) were scored once.
  • Model and threshold chosen on train only, with cross-validation grouped by creator.
  • 95% intervals resample whole creators (1,000 draws).
  • Labels miss some paid posts, so test precision is a floor.

What does the daily table do?

One row per post: proof tier, paid status, model score (only with no proof), and organic views with method and confidence. The pipeline and its tests are in the repo. It is not scheduled yet: it needs a Snowflake service account and a schema to write to.

Sources and terms

Organic truth: BIRA observation time series (public = Nimble, opt-in = private API). Paid: unified paid table and EDW Meta and TikTok ad tables. Data read 2026-10-07. Typical = median. Middle half = 25th–75th percentile. Error = (estimate − opt-in) ÷ opt-in.
